> See full documentation in [docs/design.md](file:///C:/Users/SDX30/Desktop/clg-chatbot/docs/design.md) and [docs/theme.md](file:///C:/Users/SDX30/Desktop/clg-chatbot/docs/theme.md).

## Core Principles
1. **Calm, High-Contrast Typography**: Inter sans-serif stack paired with JetBrains Mono for citations and code.
2. **Monochrome Rhythm**: Light and dark themes crafted from pure grayscale, black, and white values.
3. **Structured Hierarchy**: Spacing, weight, and subtle borders govern visual groupings without colorful clutter.
4. **Full WCAG 2.1 AA Accessibility**: High-contrast ratios, keyboard focus rings, and screen-reader semantics.
